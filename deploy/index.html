Origin & Matcha Standard

A better matcha base before the functional upgrade.

Before we add L-theanine or allulose, the formula starts with shaded matcha selected for color, aroma, and daily drinkability. We look for a vibrant green cup, a smooth mouthfeel, and a cleaner matcha taste that can work with water, milk, or oat milk.

From leaf to powder

01

Selected Tea Garden Bases

Rather than relying on a single field, the matcha base is selected from tea garden sources that can support consistent color, aroma, and taste.

02

Shaded Spring Leaves

Tea leaves are shaded before harvest to support deeper green color and a softer matcha character.

03

Steamed & Refined

Fresh leaves are steamed to preserve color and freshness, then dried, cut, screened, and refined to reduce coarse stems and veins.

04

Finely Milled Matcha

The refined leaf material is milled into a fine powder for a smoother texture and easier daily drinking.
